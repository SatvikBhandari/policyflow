How policies work

  • Every active policy that matches a request adds its approvers. The request needs all of them.
  • Amount ranges include the minimum and exclude the maximum.
  • Managers and Department Heads approve for the requester's own department. Finance Officers can be from any department.
  • If no policy matches, the request goes to the Department Head.
  • Every change is recorded in the audit log.
